Equipment marks and the weight split.

- Each exercise can now say what you lift with: a barbell, two dumbbells or one dumbbell. Set it once per exercise in the card menu under Edit exercise, in the new Equipment field. Every exercise starts with none.
- Set rows show a small mark before the weight, a bar for a barbell, two stacked dumbbells for a pair and one dumbbell for a single, so 12 kg of hammer curls and 6 kg of one-arm rows read right without a tap.
- The panel splits the total under the weight: `6 kg each` for two dumbbells, `7 kg a side · 20 kg bar` for a barbell, and `just the 20 kg bar` when there are no plates. Each barbell exercise has its own bar weight, 20 kg unless you change it, so an EZ bar can be lighter.
- The weight you log is still the total, so history, the e1RM, volume and the plus and minus steps are unchanged.
