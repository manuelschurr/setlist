Plate counts are back, and optional.

- Your plates lists a row per size: tick the ones you own, and give a count only where you could run short, such as your heaviest. A ticked size without a count reads `any` and is used as often as the weight needs. A count limits each end to its share: it takes four of a size to put one on each end of a dumbbell pair, and two for a barbell.
- Sizes you picked in 0.6.1 stay ticked, without counts. Counts entered in 0.6.0 were dropped by 0.6.1, so enter the ones that matter again.
- Barbells keep drawing their plates after the bar, from the same list.
