Adjustable dumbbells, their plates, and a new panel.

- Dumbbells can now have a handle weight, for adjustable ones that take plates. Set it in the card menu under Edit exercise, in the new Handle weight field that shows when the equipment is dumbbells. Leave it empty for fixed dumbbells.
- With a handle, the panel splits each dumbbell into plates and handle, such as `7 kg each (4.5 + 2.5 kg)` for 14 kg on 2.5 kg handles.
- Count the plates you own once in Your plates, in the workout screen's menu. The panel then draws the plates for one end of the dumbbell under the weight, heaviest inside, using the fewest plates you have. A weight your plates can't make says `not with your plates`.
- The panel's buttons are now one row per field, with minus, the value and plus joined together, so each pair of buttons sits on the number it changes. The buttons are larger and the panel is no taller than before.
- The weight you log is still the total, so history, the e1RM and volume are unchanged.
