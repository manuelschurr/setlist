Plates on the barbell, and no more counting.

- Barbells now draw their plates too: the panel shows the bar, such as `20 kg bar`, then the plates for one side, heaviest inside, from the same plates as your dumbbells.
- Your plates no longer asks how many of each you own. Tap the sizes you have, and the panel uses as many of each as the weight needs. Sizes you counted in 0.6.0 stay picked.
- The plates are drawn a little narrower, so up to six plates a side fit, which covers every load up to 155 kg on a 20 kg bar.
